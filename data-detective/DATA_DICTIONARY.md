# Synthetic data dictionary

All names, records, organisations and claims in Evidence Desk are fictional. The data were authored for this lesson on 2026-09-10 and do not represent people or research findings.

## Graph framing dataset

Six records describe fictional Luma School's monthly recycling rate. `month` is Jan–Jun and `value` is percentage points: 62, 64, 63, 66, 67 and 68. The mean is 65.0%. Axis controls change only the rendering range.

## Sampling population

The population is 200 labelled cards: 100 `North` and 100 `South`. `yes` marks support for the fictional proposal. North contains 70 yes cards; South contains 30. The population is therefore 50% yes. Simple random sampling shuffles all cards and selects without replacement. Lobby-biased sampling deliberately draws 80% from North and 20% from South, separately without replacement, then shuffles the result. The latter models unequal recruitment opportunities.

## Correlation records

Eight fictional puzzle-learner records contain `course` (`Intro` or `Advanced`), `sessions` (weekly practice sessions) and `score` (points). Intro records are (5,55), (6,57), (7,59), (8,61); Advanced records are (2,80), (3,82), (4,84), (5,86). Mixing the groups produces a negative association while each group trends upward. This constructed reversal exposes `course` as a plausible third variable. It does not establish any causal effect.
